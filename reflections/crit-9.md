# Reflections

1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?
The focus of this week's crit was on handling changes across several users. This depends on establishing a clear idea of how the design could support this interaction. As this is a productivity app, I wanted to minimise the amount of interaction required from users. I therefore prioritised simple, productive interaction points, such as voting for 'focus time'.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?
This crit highlights the importance to the project specifications. Establishing clear criteria from the beginning helps me stay focused on the essential requirements throughout the development process. I realised that this approach is far more effective than jumping straight into implementation without a clear direction, which can lead to wasted effort.
